### Note on Western Digital (you asked about it)

WDC *does* pass this screen (high ROE, 20x P/E, $173B). But storage (NAND/HDD) is
**cyclical**, and cyclicals have *low P/E at the top of the cycle and high P/E at
the bottom*. A 20x P/E on peak-cycle earnings is not "cheap" — it's expensive on a
cycle-adjusted basis. That's exactly why it's in the screen, and exactly why it
doesn't belong in a 10-year buy-and-forget sleeve. Great trade, wrong sleeve.

## Sizing + discipline (non-negotiable)

1. Explorer sleeve = **5–10% of total**, split across **4–6 names minimum**.
2. **No single name > 2%** of the whole portfolio.
3. Same monthly-SIP discipline as the core. Review quarterly, not daily.
4. **Pre-agreed exit, decided now while unemotional:** if the sleeve underperforms
   the core index for **3 years running, shut it down** and fold it back into the index.
5. The "sell if" column above is the *only* reason to sell. Price going down is
   **not** a reason. Price going up is **not** a reason.
