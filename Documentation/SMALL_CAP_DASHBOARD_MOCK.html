How this bot works

This bot follows fixed rules. It never guesses. It BUYS one lot of a small company when the price is above its long-term trend, the medium trend is above the long trend, a fresh buy signal has just printed — and the company passes a health check (makes a profit, low debt, decent returns). It SELLS using a "safety line" that sits 2% below the highest price seen since buying. The line only moves up. When price closes at or below the line, it sells half; it sells the rest if price falls another 3%. It will NEVER sell a piece for less than 2% above what you paid (after charges). If the safety line is below that level, it just holds and waits. There is NO stop-loss, on purpose. A sudden fall can still catch it before it sells — that is the cost of never selling at a loss. Money: ₹ 10,000 per stock, one position per company, up to 3 companies.
