Blocked on the four Step-0 answers (Zerodha account · Kite keys · run-1 budget · first fundamentals.csv) to flip orders_enabled; everything up to log-only trading is built · review: REVIEW.html (lead + dad-UX findings; the gross-vs-net floor is the one open item and is deferred to broker.py by design)

One line: scan small-caps → keep only fundamentally-good names → rank → buy the top few (₹10,000 each) on Zerodha delivery → trail a 2% profit rope and sell in two pieces on the way down → never sell a piece for less than +2%; otherwise hold.
The +2% floor is a net-of-cost price (costs.py → net_floor_price): a zero cost config = the old gross number, a realistic one (STT 0.1, 40/300 bps slip) lifts the break-even sell to ≈ +5.8%. The runner (step 5) feeds the live cost inputs.

1. What this is (and what it isn't)

A reusable strategy framework — new strategies plug in as one file each — running this small-cap momentum strategy as strategy #1, with a browser page B's father can read.

It started as an experiment ("does a stop-loss help?"). It is now a production strategy with a small risk budget: the exit rule is a soft stop (the trailing rope), and the "buy only good companies" rule is a hard requirement, not a variable. The old stop-loss comparison survives only as a dashboard diagnostic (§9).

2. Money & risk — plain terms

How many stocks
total_budget ÷ per_stock_amount, capped. ₹30,000 ÷ ₹10,000 = 3 names. Raise total_budget to ₹100,000 → 10 names. Hard cap max_names_cap can never be exceeded.
Per stock
₹10,000 full position. Optional extra ceiling: pct_of_account_cap (2% of the account).
Selling — the rope
Once a stock is up, a "rope" sits 2% below its highest price so far and only ever moves up. When price falls back and touches the rope, the bot sells half now. The second half's sell price is locked the instant the first half fires — at (fill price of half 1 − 3%) — and does not follow the rope after that. It's a fixed resting target, not a moving one.
The +2% floor
The bot will not sell a piece for less than the buy price + 2% (after costs). If the rope is below that floor, the bot just holds and waits.
Stranded half
If the second piece's sell price would be below the +2% floor: with exit.strand_final_half=true (default) that half is held and the dashboard flags STRANDED HALF — manual sell?; set it false and the bot sells the final half at its trigger anyway. B's choice, in config; the banner states which is active.
Stop-loss
None. hard_stop_pct exists but is OFF. never_sell_at_loss is ON.
Honest caveat
"Never sell at a loss" is an intention. A small-cap can gap straight through the rope and the floor overnight (circuit-locked, no buyer). Then you hold at a loss with no exit rule left. The rope works on daily closes — it is best-effort, not a guarantee.
Order type
CNC delivery only. No leverage, no intraday auto-square-off.

3. Isolation from the live bot

Live ARUN RSI bot stays on mStock. This strategy runs on a dedicated Zerodha account (B holds 2 Zerodha + 1 mStock on purpose). They share nothing — not the database, not the broker, not the login token.

Own DB database/strategies.db. Never writes to trades.db. Zero edits and zero imports of kickstart.py / risk_manager.py / trades_db.py (the live risk sweep auto-sells any position it sees in trades.db, ignoring the strategy tag — so this strategy must stay out of that file entirely).

4. Broker reality — Zerodha

Not fully headless. Zerodha's access token dies ~6am daily. One manual tap + paste per trading day: the bot shows an "Authorize" link, someone opens it, pastes the redirected URL back; the token then lasts the day and survives restarts. Someone has to do this each morning.

Step 0 — decide before build step 4

  1. Which Zerodha account is this strategy's? Named in config; the other account stays untouched.
  2. Kite Connect app created on it. Order placement is on the free Personal plan — you do not need the paid tier to place, modify or cancel CNC orders. The ₹500/month Connect plan only adds real-time WebSocket ticks and historical candle downloads; skip it until the strategy proves it needs intraday reaction (a once-a-day bot doesn't). Historical candles for the one-off backtest come free from NSE bhavcopy / nselib.

SituationWhat the bot does

Price collapses fast (no stop-loss)No automatic exit below the floor. Manual decision.
No live quote for N days (circuit-locked for weeks / suspended / delisted)Bot does not silently keep holding. Dashboard flags DEAD NAME — manual review so dad decides. Config knob: dead_name_days.

Rope = highest price since entry × (1 − 2%). Ratchets up only. Evaluated on daily closes — it can overshoot on an overnight gap.

8. Fundamentals data

Primary
Curated fundamentals.csv — ~250 rows (ROE, debt/equity, earnings sign, market cap, avg daily value), refreshed by hand once a quarter. Fundamentals barely move between quarters. 100% coverage, nothing to break.
Single source of truth
This CSV — not a hard-coded ticker list — defines which names are in play and the market-cap band. The universe filter and the fundamentals gate both read the band from here, so they can't disagree (no more "passes the gate but the universe excludes it").
Fill-in
yfinance auto-fills any field it has.
Still blank
Stock excluded + logged ("skipped N names — couldn't verify health"). Dad sees the count.
Don't
Scrape screener.in / Tickertape — against their terms, breaks without warning, gets IP-blocked. A human doing the quarterly export is fine; a script is not.
Paid upgrade
If the quarterly CSV becomes a chore: EODHD (~$20/month, best Indian small-cap coverage, legal to automate).

9. Costs & the day-14 diagnostic

Costs run on every simulated fill, buy and sell. Zerodha delivery: ₹0 brokerage, but not zero charges — the model includes STT 0.1%/side, stamp duty 0.015% (buy side), exchange txn + SEBI + GST ≈ 0.006%/side, plus DP charges on sells. Small per fill, but material against a 2% floor over hundreds of fills — so "+2% net" is honest, not optimistic. Entry slippage ~40 bps; a thinning small-cap exit is modelled at ~300 bps or "no fill", not a flat number. The +2% floor check runs per piece on real post-cost proceeds.

Day-14 comparison is now just a diagnostic panel: "how would a hard stop at 5% / 8% / 10% have changed this strategy's result." The scoreboard shows the trade count n and says "too small to conclude" until n ≥ 30 AND the universe is expanded to Nifty Smallcap 250.

10. Dashboard (for dad)

One page served by the existing backend at http://localhost:8000/strategy — no npm, no build. Six views:

  1. Today's scan — ranked candidates, with the score "why" (MACD, trend, RSI, recency)
  2. Fundamentals — market cap, P/E, ROE, debt/equity, revenue growth, sector, 52-week range, dividend yield, promoter %, 4-quarter revenue & profit trend
  3. Positions — held, entry / now / P&L, days held, days red, highest price, MAE, rope value, STRANDED flag
  4. Activity — plain English: "BUY 12 XYZ @ ₹340 — MACD cross yesterday, above 200-EMA"
  5. How it works — the strategy's own description, no code
  6. Scoreboard + the day-14 diagnostic

Plus a kill switch and a per-strategy on/off toggle.

11. What this does NOT do

  • Does not touch or import the live ARUN RSI bot or its database.
  • Does not use leverage or intraday trading — delivery only.
  • Does not guarantee "never at a loss" — an overnight gap can beat the rope.
  • Does not run itself at 6am — someone taps the Zerodha login link each day.
  • Does not scrape fundamentals sites.
  • Does not place a real order until Phase A is fixed, the log-only days are clean, and the IP is whitelisted.
  • Does not bring the desktop app's full feature set to the web.
